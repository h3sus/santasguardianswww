Good to know

Quick answers to what people usually ask before booking.

Is it safe?

Yes. The weapons are lightweight and padded, and everyone wears a helmet that protects the head and face. Like in any sport, the worst that usually happens is a small bruise or a twisted ankle. We have extra insurance for participants, but please have your own insurance in order too.

Do I need to be fit or sporty?

No. You set your own pace and can take it easy or go all in. Anyone who would rather stay out of the wildest battles can take another role, such as referee or a VIP to protect, so everyone gets to join.

Who can join?

The minimum age is 12, and we recommend that young fighters have an adult with them. There is no upper age limit. Each boot camp is for 4–8 people, and for bigger groups you can contact us. If someone in your group has a disability or physical limitation, contact us in advance and we will see what we can do.

What should I wear?

Dress warmly. Too much is better than too little: you can always take a layer off, but you can't add one you didn't bring. Wear good shoes that keep your ankles dry in deeper snow, and bring warm gloves. We provide all the equipment. You will probably get sweaty, so it is a good idea to plan time for a sauna or shower afterwards.

What if the weather is bad?

Everything happens outdoors, and we don't cancel because of the weather. The Guardians are quite tough like that. Just dress for the conditions.

Do we have to act or be in character?

No, unless you want to. Come as you are. The main thing is to enjoy moving and playing together. If you feel like getting into character, go for it, just be nice to others and play by the rules.

Where do we meet?

We come and escort you 10 minutes before your session from the SantaPark bus stop or Hostel Ibedcity. It is a 5-minute walk from both. You choose your pickup spot when booking. See the map.

Can I cancel my booking?

Bookings are non-refundable and all sales are final.
